# 🤖 AI Paper Checker (Smart-Scan & Grade)

An innovative, AI-powered exam paper checking assistant designed specifically for educators. This project aims to eliminate the hectic process of manual data entry and individual image uploads by introducing a **"Note Counting Machine"** style continuous scanning experience.

---

## 🚀 The Vision 

Manual paper correction and grading is a massive administrative burden for teachers. Existing software solutions require teachers to manually photograph and upload each student's answer sheet one by one, which is time-consuming and exhausting.

Our solution introduces a **Continuous Stream/Video Scanning** model. Teachers just place their smartphone on a stand and flip pages under the camera sequentially—just like a currency counting machine. The AI handles the rest in the background.

---

## ✨ Key Features 

* **Continuous Live Scanning:** No more clicking photos one by one. Flip pages naturally, and the AI captures frames automatically.
* **Intelligent Handwriting Recognition (OCR):** Powered by Advanced AI Agents to read and understand handwritten student answers.
* **Automated Grading & Multi-Step Reasoning:** Leverages AI reasoning to evaluate answers against an answer key and assign fair scores.
* **Seamless LMS/Excel Integration:** Automatically syncs final grades directly to school databases or spreadsheets.
* **Future Hardware Vision:** A plug-and-play dedicated scanning device for schools (Hardware-as-a-Service model).
